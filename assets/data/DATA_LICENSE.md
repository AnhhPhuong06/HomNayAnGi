# OpenStreetMap dining catalog

The derived files `real_restaurants.json` and `catalog_manifest.json` contain
OpenStreetMap data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under ODbL 1.0.

- Attribution / copyright: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- License: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- Extract provider: https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/vietnam.html
- Reproduction: `tool/data/import_osm.py` and `tool/data/requirements.txt`.

The JSON catalog is distributed in editable form alongside the app. Source object
URLs and snapshot provenance are retained. This data license does not change the
license of the app's independent source code, or cover third-party TikTok videos.

`restaurants.json` is the separate synthetic demo fixture and is not mixed into
the open-data catalog. No Google Maps ratings, photos or reviews are copied into it.

## Photographs

Photographs in `assets/venue_photos/` retain their individual Commons licenses, separately from the ODbL catalog. See `assets/venue_photos/CREDITS.md` and `assets/data/restaurant_photos.json` for author, license, source and capture date.
